Trash & recycling

Find your pickup day, what goes where, and how to schedule bulky items.

The basics

  • Bag all trash and put it inside your City-issued barrel. Loose trash is not collected.
  • Put barrels at the curb no earlier than 4 p.m. the day before pickup. Nothing in the street.
  • Separate cardboard for recycling. Trash placed in cardboard boxes is not collected.
  • Yard waste goes out on trash day, April to early December, in paper compost bags or marked barrels. No plastic bags. Branches under 3 inches wide and 3 feet long, tied in bundles.
  • Keep dogs leashed or fenced on collection day.

Bulky items and fees

  • Schedule all bulky pickups first by calling (401) 728-0500, ext. 233.
  • Furniture: up to 3 pieces a week. Love seats, couches, sectionals (up to 3 pieces), kitchen tables and chairs (up to 6) are $10 each.
  • Refrigerators, freezers, mattresses and box springs: $16 each.
  • No charge for stoves, washers, dryers, water coolers, TVs, or up to 2 air conditioners a year (then $16 each).
  • Propane tanks: Transfer Station, 240 Grotto Avenue. Free up to 20 lbs, $22 over 20 lbs.
  • Rugs larger than 5' x 7' count as construction material and go to the Transfer Station.

Not collected at the curb

Construction and demolition material (dirt, rocks, lumber, concrete, tile, toilets, tubs, cabinets), auto parts, warm ashes, frozen containers, uncooked animal parts, and full paint cans. Let latex paint dry and put the open can in the trash; for oil paint call the Eco Depot at (401) 942-1430.
