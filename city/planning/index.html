Planning & Redevelopment

Housing, development review, historic districts, grants and major projects.

Planning and Redevelopment works to make Pawtucket a great place to live, work and visit. It reviews development, encourages economic growth, and runs State and Federal grant programs including CDBG and HOME. Walk-in hours are Monday – Friday, 9 a.m. – noon; appointments outside those hours are available on request.
