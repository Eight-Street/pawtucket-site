Substance Abuse Prevention

Prevention, treatment and recovery resources in and around Pawtucket.

In a crisis involving substance use, call BH Link at (401) 414-5465, 24/7. In an emergency, call 911.

Get help

  • Find treatment and recovery support: Prevent Overdose RI
  • Youth mental health: Kids Link, (855) 543-5465, bhlink.org
  • Opioid treatment in Pawtucket: BHG, 82 Pond Street, (401) 727-1287
  • Walk-in access: Gateway Walk-In Access, Pawtucket
  • Recovery community: RICARES
  • State resources: RI Department of Health
