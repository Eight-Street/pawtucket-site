Veterans

Benefits, tax exemptions and support for Pawtucket veterans.

Thank you for your service. The City offers property tax exemptions for eligible veterans (see exemptions).

Where to get help

  • VA liaison at the Senior Center: first Thursday of each month, 9 a.m. – 2:30 p.m., by appointment. Call (401) 728-7582.
  • RI Office of Veterans Affairs: (401) 921-2119.
  • Pawtucket Veterans Council: organizes Veterans Day and Memorial Day ceremonies and connects veterans' groups. Call (401) 728-0500 ext. 281 or email MayorsOffice@pawtucketri.com.
  • Honor Flight: World War II, Korean War and terminally ill veterans may be eligible for a trip to the memorials in Washington, D.C. Contact the Mayor's Office.
