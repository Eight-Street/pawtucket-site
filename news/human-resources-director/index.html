Now hiring: Human Resources Director

Nov 10, 2026 · Jobs

Full-time, exempt. Salary: $95,000 – $120,000.

The City of Pawtucket is looking for an experienced, collaborative Human Resources Director to lead all of the City's HR operations, supporting a diverse municipal workforce and shaping workplace culture, development and engagement. The Director reports to the Director of Finance and advises City leadership.

What you'll do

  • Lead HR staff and operations.
  • Direct recruitment, hiring, onboarding and separations.
  • Run benefits, including health, dental, retirement, workers' compensation and leave programs.
  • Manage employee and labor relations, investigations, grievances and arbitration support.
  • Develop HR policies, performance management, training and wellness programs.
  • Oversee Equal Employment Opportunity and diversity programs.

How to apply

Email Jarkels@pawtucketri.gov. See all openings on the Work for the City page.
