Now hiring: Director of Finance

Nov 10, 2026 · Jobs

Full-time executive leadership position. Pay range: $115,000 – $140,000.

The Director of Finance reports directly to the Mayor and leads the City's financial strategy and operations: budgeting, accounting, payroll, purchasing, treasury, tax collection, assessment, information technology and human resources.

What you'll do

  • Advise the Mayor and City leadership on all financial matters.
  • Lead the annual operating and capital budgets.
  • Oversee Finance, Accounting, Collections, HR, IT, Tax Assessment and Purchasing.
  • Manage the City's ERP system (currently MUNIS) and coordinate annual audits.
  • Support the City Council with financial analysis and recommendations.

How to apply

Email Jarkles@pawtucketri.gov. See all openings on the Work for the City page.
