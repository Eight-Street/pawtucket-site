City Council meeting moves to Tuesday, November 24

Oct 21, 2026 · Notice

The regular meetings of the Pawtucket City Council and the Board of License Commissioners scheduled for Wednesday, November 25, 2026, have been rescheduled to Tuesday, November 24, 2026, because of the Thanksgiving holiday.

Meeting times stay the same: the Board of License Commissioners at 6:45 p.m., followed by the City Council at 7:00 p.m.

Agendas are posted on the RI Secretary of State open meetings site.
