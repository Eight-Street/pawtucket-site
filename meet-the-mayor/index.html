Meet the Mayor

Draft in the Mayor's voice, for his review

I grew up behind the counter of my family's corner deli. I started at 14, stocking shelves and cutting onions, and my parents made it clear I'd earn my place like anyone else on the payroll. That's where I learned that no job is too small, and that a business, like a city, runs on people showing up for each other.

After college I joined Teach For America and taught middle-school social studies in Camden, New Jersey. My students and I restarted the student government, started a school newspaper, and organized clean-up days with families in the neighborhood. Those kids taught me that people rise to what's expected of them, and that institutions should rise to meet people too.

Erin, a fellow Teach For America alum and a Rhode Islander, and I chose Pawtucket to raise our family when our first child was born in 2009. We fell for the walkable neighborhoods, the schools, the parks and the arts, and most of all the people: a diverse city where neighbors look out for one another.

I've spent my career in Rhode Island's nonprofit sector, working on education, housing, affordability and community. I've helped expand afterschool and summer programs, supported the state's affordable housing bonds, and grown workforce training partnerships. I earned my doctorate in education while working full time, and for the past nine years I've led a statewide community organization through growth and through hard moments, including the pandemic.

As your mayor, I'm focused on our schools, housing and affordability, health and community, and on a City Hall that works as hard as the people it serves. This website is one small piece of that. If something isn't working, tell us. I'm listening.

Adam Greenman

Adam Greenman, Mayor of Pawtucket
