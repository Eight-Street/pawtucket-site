Meet Mayor-elect Adam Greenman

A Pawtucket parent, educator and nonprofit leader, ready to make City Hall work hard for the people it serves.

Photo of
Mayor-elect Greenman

Draft bio adapted from the Mayor-elect's transition site, for his team's review.

Raised behind the counter

Adam grew up working in his family's corner deli. He started at 14, stocking shelves and doing whatever needed doing, and his parents held him to the same standard as every other employee. That's where he learned that no task is beneath anyone and that a business, like a city, runs on people showing up for each other.

A teacher first

After college, Adam joined Teach For America and taught middle-school social studies in Camden, New Jersey. In the classroom he went beyond the curriculum: restarting student government, launching a school newspaper and organizing neighborhood clean-ups with students and families.

Why Pawtucket

Adam and his wife, Erin, a fellow Teach For America alum and Rhode Islander, chose Pawtucket to raise their family when their first child was born in 2009. They were drawn by the city's walkable neighborhoods, its schools, parks and cultural life, and most of all by the diversity and close-knit spirit of its people.

A career building community

Adam has spent his career in Rhode Island's nonprofit sector, working on education, housing, affordability and community building. He helped expand afterschool and summer programs, supported state affordable-housing bonds and grew workforce training partnerships. He earned a doctorate in education while working full time, and for the past nine years he has led a statewide community organization through growth and difficult moments, including the COVID-19 pandemic.

What he'll focus on

Education, housing and affordability, health, and community, along with restoring pride in Pawtucket and a City Hall that works as hard as its residents.
